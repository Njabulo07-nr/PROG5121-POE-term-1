**Chosen Client Entity:** Souitro's Art Studio

## Project Overview
Souitro's Art is a digital illustration studio and creative agency based in Pretoria, South Africa. The studio specializes in custom character portraits, comic-style brand logos, and digital web graphics. This website serves as an online showcase and business portal designed to display portfolio artwork, provide transparent service pricing, and streamline commission requests from clients and potential commercial sponsors.

---

## Website Goals & Objectives
* **Showcase Portfolio:** Present digital artwork across multiple categories to attract potential personal and business clients.
* **Lead Generation:** Enable users to request custom artwork quotes via an interactive commission enquiry form.
* **Establish Online Presence:** Provide official contact details and multiple studio locations for physical and remote consultations.
* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
  * Increase monthly quote submissions via the enquiry form.
  * Improve user session duration across portfolio and service pages.
  * Maintain dynamic cross-device responsiveness and accessibility standards.

---

## Key Features & Functionality
* **Semantic Structure:** Native HTML5 layout (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`) across all pages.
* **Global Navigation:** Accessible, standard navigation menu included on every web page.
* **Structured Pricing Table:** Itemized breakdown of artwork types, turnaround times, and commission rates.
* **Commission Enquiry Form:** Multi-field HTML form with input validation for service types, budget estimates, and deadlines.
* **Multi-Location Contact Details:** Physical office listings for Pretoria Main Studio and Cape Town Satellite Office.
